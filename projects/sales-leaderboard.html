Case study

Live Sales Leaderboard

A sales team's leaderboard was updated manually, with multiple hands involved. I replaced it with a Power BI report that cycles the top five salespeople across the office TVs, so the whole team sees current numbers every day without anyone touching a spreadsheet. Management now pays a monthly bonus to everyone on the board.

Rebuilt on synthetic data. The original was built for a client, so every company name, person and number on this page is made up. The problem and the approach are real.
